# Formula 1 Race Car

Interactive 3D surface mesh of a high-performance open-wheel race car.

---
## Aerodynamics & Surface Geometry

The chassis features:
- Front splitter and multi-element rear wing for downforce management
- Sculpted sidepod air intakes and cockpit halo structure
- Low-drag wheel geometry and diffusers

Rendered in WebGL2 / WebXR using the static **crystal** shader preset.
